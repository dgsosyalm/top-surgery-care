Ftm Surgery FAQS
What is involved in female-to-male gender surgery?

Female-to-male surgery typically includes procedures such as mastectomy (removal of breast tissue), phalloplasty or metoidioplasty (construction of a male genitalia), and, in some cases, hysterectomy (removal of the uterus and ovaries). Each procedure is tailored to meet the patient's goals and medical needs.

How long is the recovery time after FTM surgery?

Recovery time varies depending on the procedures performed. For a mastectomy, it usually takes 2–4 weeks for initial recovery, while full healing may take several months. For phalloplasty or metoidioplasty, recovery can take several weeks to months, as these are more complex surgeries.

Will I be able to achieve normal sexual function after surgery?

Sexual function after surgery depends on the type of procedure performed. Phalloplasty and metoidioplasty aim to create functional genitalia, and patients may achieve sensation and the ability to engage in sexual activity. However, achieving full function may require additional procedures, such as penile implants.

Are the results permanent, and can the appearance be further refined?

The results of FTM surgeries are permanent. However, revision surgeries may be performed if the patient desires additional refinements to the appearance or function. Regular follow-ups with a healthcare provider are essential to ensure the best outcomes.

2025 Ftm Surgery Tips
Research Experienced Surgeons: Choose a surgeon with extensive experience in transgender surgeries to ensure the best possible outcome and avoid complications.
Understand the Process: Be well-informed about the procedure, recovery time, and potential risks before undergoing surgery to make an informed decision.
Mental Health Support: Seek counseling or support groups during the decision-making process and throughout recovery to help with emotional well-being.
Plan for Aftercare: Arrange for post-surgery care, including follow-up appointments, medication, and support during recovery.
Realistic Expectations: Understand that while surgery can significantly enhance physical appearance, it's important to have realistic expectations about the results and healing process.
