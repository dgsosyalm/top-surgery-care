# About

Transgender Surgery Consultant

Welcome to Top Surgery Turkey, your trusted destination for gender affirmation procedures, particularly focused on female-to-male (FTM) top surgery. Dr. Serkan Dinar, we are dedicated to providing exceptional surgical solutions for individuals on their gender transition journey.

With years of expertise and a deep understanding of our patients' needs, Dr. Serkan Dinar combines advanced medical techniques with an empathetic approach to deliver life-changing results. At Top Surgery Turkey, we pride ourselves on creating a safe, inclusive, and supportive environment for everyone who walks through our doors.

Our mission is to empower individuals by aligning their physical appearance with their true identity. From the initial consultation to post-operative care, we ensure that every step of your journey is handled with the utmost professionalism, respect, and confidentiality.

Located in the heart of Turkey, we are proud to welcome patients from around the globe. Choose Top Surgery Turkey for world-class care, transformative results, and a team that truly understands and supports your goals.
